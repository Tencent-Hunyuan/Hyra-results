The centering equivalence has a short direct proof. Put p=(n-2)/2, b_k=integral_{-1}^1 q^(2k)(1-q^2)^p dq, c_k=integral_{-1}^1 z^(2k)(1-z^2)^(p+1/2)dz, d_k=integral_{-1}^1 (1-t^2)^(k+p+1/2)dt. Integrating z^(2k)(1-z^2-t^2)^p over the unit disk in the two orders gives b_k*d_k=b_0*c_k. Hence the inverse coefficient map takes zero weighted potential moment exactly to zero weighted fiber-center mean. The C2 remainder follows by a smooth radial parametrization of the sheared ball and rotating the integration domain; no spherical-harmonic inversion is needed. This remains a candidate pending fact_submit. The family is already smooth, so no smoothing or recentering assumption is required.
